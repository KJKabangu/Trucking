Driver qualification

Every driver operating under our authority is qualified before they touch a truck and monitored continuously afterward:

  • Valid Class A CDL verified against the issuing state
  • Motor vehicle record pulled at hire and reviewed annually
  • Previous employer verification for the last three years
  • DOT physical with a certified medical examiner, kept current
  • Pre-employment drug screening plus random testing in an active consortium
  • Clearinghouse queries at hire and annually thereafter

Hours of service

Our trucks run electronic logging devices as required under 49 CFR Part 395. Hours are monitored, violations are reviewed with the driver, and we do not ask a driver to run past legal hours to save an appointment. A missed appointment costs a day. The alternative can cost considerably more.

Equipment and maintenance

Tractors and trailers are on a scheduled preventive maintenance program with documented inspections. Drivers complete pre-trip and post-trip inspection reports, and any defect affecting safe operation takes the unit out of service until it is repaired.

Cargo securement

Loads are secured to the FMCSA cargo securement standards in 49 CFR Part 393 Subpart I, using load bars, straps, and blocking as the freight requires. Drivers re-check securement within the first 50 miles and at every required interval after that.

If something goes wrong

Damage or shortage should be noted on the delivery receipt before the driver leaves, photographed, and reported to us the same day. We open the claim against our cargo policy, give you a single point of contact, and stay with it until it closes. Concealed damage should be reported as soon as it is discovered.

Request documents

Certificate of insurance, W-9, carrier packet, and signed authority. Sent same business day.
