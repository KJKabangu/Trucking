Who we are

What we collect

We collect two kinds of information.

Information you give us. When you submit the quote form, the driver application, or the contact form, we receive what you typed into it. That typically includes your name, company, email address, phone number, and details about a shipment or your driving history.

Information collected automatically. Our hosting provider records standard server logs including IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps. If analytics are enabled, an analytics provider may set cookies or similar identifiers to measure aggregate site usage.

Why we use it

  • To prepare and send freight rate quotes you asked for
  • To arrange, dispatch, and complete transportation services
  • To evaluate driver applications and communicate with applicants
  • To respond to questions, claims, and billing matters
  • To keep records required of motor carriers by federal and state regulation
  • To protect the site against spam and abuse

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

When we share information

We disclose information only in these situations:

  • Service providers. Our website host, form processor, and email provider handle data on our behalf so the site can function.
  • Completing your shipment. Details necessary to pick up and deliver freight are shared with the assigned driver and with consignees, shippers, or brokers involved in that shipment.
  • Legal and regulatory. When required by law, subpoena, or a lawful request from a regulator such as the FMCSA or DOT.
  • Insurance and claims. With our insurers and their adjusters when a cargo or liability claim is filed.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies for advertising. If web analytics are enabled, the analytics provider may set a cookie to distinguish repeat visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings without losing the ability to use this site.

How long we keep it

Quote requests and correspondence are retained as long as needed for the business relationship and for the record-retention periods that apply to motor carriers. Driver applications are retained in line with DOT recordkeeping requirements. You may ask us to delete information that we are not legally required to keep.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS and form submissions are encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send sensitive identifiers such as a Social Security number through the website forms.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email {{ contact.email }} and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy law, and we will honor those rights where they apply.

Children

This site is for business use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
