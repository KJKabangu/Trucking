Before you tender

What we need to quote accurately

A rate is only as good as the information behind it. These six details prevent almost every surprise charge in freight.

Full addresses

Pickup and delivery with contact names and phone numbers. A ZIP-to-ZIP rate stops being accurate the moment a receiver turns out to be somewhere else.

Commodity and weight

What it is and what it weighs. Weight drives legality, not just price, and a load that scales heavy at the first weigh station costs everyone a day.

Dimensions or pallet count

Pallet count and whether they can stack, or linear feet for partials. Oversized single pieces need length, width, and height so we know which truck can take them.

Appointment windows

Hard appointments, FCFS windows, and hours at both ends. Detention gets expensive fastest when nobody mentioned the receiver closes at two.

Special handling

Liftgate, driver assist, inside delivery, pallet exchange, limited dock access, or residential delivery. Each one is a real cost, so we quote it up front instead of billing it later.

Value and paperwork

Declared value if it exceeds our standard cargo coverage, plus any BOL, seal, or documentation requirements your receiver enforces.

Straight answers

What we will not haul

A carrier that says yes to everything is telling you something. These are outside what we do well, and we would rather point you somewhere better than learn that lesson with your freight on our trailer.

  • Refrigerated and temperature-controlled freight. We run dry van and box truck only
  • Open-deck freight needing a flatbed or step deck
  • Household goods and personal moves, which require separate authority
  • Live animals
  • Oversize or overweight loads needing permits and escorts
  • Freight requiring hazmat endorsements we do not carry

If your load falls in one of these, call anyway. After {{ $.years }} years we know who does handle it.
